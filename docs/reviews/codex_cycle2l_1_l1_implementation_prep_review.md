## L1 Schema-Free Contract Review

PASS.

The DEI-candidate contract is concrete enough to prevent ad-hoc hidden mapping while still remaining outside the findings schema. The document correctly positions DEI candidates as upstream, document-level intake material produced outside plugin core and consumed by the Skill, not as direct renderer/validator input.

The contract preserves the right boundaries:

- No `judgment_code` or `judgment_label` is generated by the intake/DEI layer.
- `extraction_quality`, `needs_ocr`, and priority hints are triage signals, not KSSB judgments.
- Source excerpts are preserved rather than synthesized.
- Failure states are explicit rather than silently producing partial evidence.
- Existing findings schema remains the only structured report contract.

The plan also correctly defers structured confidence, bbox, or `needs_ocr` fields in findings to a separate schema-evolution decision if implementation proves that necessary.

## Hint / Routing / Test Plan Review

PASS.

The page/bbox/quality convention is a safe compromise:

- Findings receive human-readable location hints such as `p.<page> - <section>`.
- Bbox coordinates remain in DEI-level material and are not smuggled into findings as a hidden schema.
- `source_documents.notes` is limited to document-level quality notes.
- The convention helps human reviewers locate evidence without changing the findings schema.

The Skill routing draft aligns with the current Skill, schema, and validator:

- Low-confidence, unreadable, skipped-image, or OCR-needed areas are routed to `not_verifiable`.
- `not_verifiable` continues to require `missing_info` and `customer_questions`.
- The draft avoids turning matching failure or unreadable pages into a "not disclosed" conclusion.
- It preserves Source-bound Analysis and human-review boundaries.

The 2L-2 test plan is enough for implementation entry. It covers existing core tests, new DEI producer determinism, detection-to-priority hints, no judgment generation, original excerpt preservation, schema-valid findings output, and boundary tests preventing direct DEI-to-renderer/validator use.
